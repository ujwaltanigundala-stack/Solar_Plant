Hardware Discrepancy

While both facilities generate up to ~1,400 kW AC Power, their inverter string hardware architectures diverge fundamentally:

  • Plant 1: String inverters receive up to 14,471 kW DC (ratio ≈ 10:1 DC-to-AC conversion).
  • Plant 2: String inverters receive up to 1,440 kW DC (ratio ≈ 1:1 DC-to-AC conversion).

Consequently, linear regression models trained on Plant 1 learn a DC_POWER standardized weight β ≈ 402 kW. On Plant 2, this causes massive under-prediction and a transfer RMSE penalty exceeding 390 kW without local calibration.
