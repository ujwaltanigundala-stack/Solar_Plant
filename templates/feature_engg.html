Evaluating StandardScaler, MinMaxScaler, and RobustScaler transformations.

Scaling Theory

  • StandardScaler: Zero mean, unit variance $z = \frac{x - \mu}{\sigma}$. Essential for regularized $L_1/L_2$ penalties.
  • Condition Number: Mitigates catastrophic ill-conditioning from raw DC magnitude.

Regularization Benchmark

Ridge ($L_2$), Lasso ($L_1$), and Elastic Net shrinkage penalties.

1. Ridge (α=1e-4) 2. Lasso Sparsity 3. Elastic Net

Regularization Insights

  • Ridge (L2): Shrinks weights smoothly; condition number drops from $1.77 \times 10^{12}$ to stable.
  • Lasso (L1): Coordinate descent drives 8 of 10 coefficients to exactly zero.
  • Elastic Net: Convex combination of $L_1$ and $L_2$ penalties.
