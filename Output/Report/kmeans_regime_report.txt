5. PHYSICAL DOMAIN INSIGHTS:
--------------------------------------------------
1. Nighttime Inactivity Isolation: The largest cluster (~45%) corresponds exactly to nighttime,
   where solar irradiation is 0.0 and module temperature closely matches ambient temperature.
2. Separation of Solar Regimes: Daylight data naturally partitions into morning ramp-up
   (low angle, cooler modules) and peak noon generation (high thermal differential, high irradiance).
3. Speed vs Accuracy Tradeoff: MiniBatchKMeans is 3x to 6x faster while preserving >98% of
   optimal Lloyd inertia, proving effective for scaling to multi-year solar monitoring datasets.
