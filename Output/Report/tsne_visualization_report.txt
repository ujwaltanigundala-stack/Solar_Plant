3. CRITICAL METHODOLOGY CAVEATS (Slide 9 Principles):
--------------------------------------------------
1. Non-Metric Distances: Distances between separated clusters in t-SNE are arbitrary.
   t-SNE preserves local neighborhood topology, not global Euclidean distances.
2. Perplexity Sensitivity: Low perplexity (Perp=5) fragments continuous diurnal arcs into micro-islands,
   while high perplexity (Perp=50) smoothly reconstructs the continuous daylight generation curve.
3. Exploratory Tool Only: Standard t-SNE lacks an out-of-sample transform() method, meaning it
   CANNOT be used as an upstream feature extractor for live predictive inference.
