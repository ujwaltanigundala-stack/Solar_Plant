6. CRITICAL ML LIFECYCLE & GENERALIZATION INSIGHTS:
-----------------------------------------------------------------
1. High In-Plant Precision: All regularized models achieve outstanding test performance on Plant 1
   (R2 ~ 0.9996, RMSE ~ 7.78 kW), closely tracking true inverter output.
2. Ridge Multicollinearity Resolution: Ridge reduces matrix condition number, preventing collinear
   swings between IRRADIATION and DC_POWER.
3. Lasso Sparsity: Under small optimal alpha (0.0001), Lasso preserves the core physical variables
   while dampening noisy temporal signals.
4. Cross-Plant Domain Shift Discovery: Applying the Plant 1 trained model directly to Plant 2
   without adjusting for the ~10x difference in DC_POWER reporting units yields negative R2.
   This precisely answers our central research question: Successful in-plant regression does NOT
   guarantee plug-and-play cross-plant generalization without domain-level normalization or retraining.
