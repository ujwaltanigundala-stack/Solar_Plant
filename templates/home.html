Welcome to the Solar Power Prediction and Generalization intelligence platform. This application analyzes synchronized 15-minute sensor feeds from 22 grid-connected inverters across 34 continuous days, models electrical solar generation (AC_POWER in kW) using multivariate regression (OLS, Ridge, Lasso, Elastic Net), discovers physical operational regimes through unsupervised clustering, and evaluates cross-plant domain transfer from Plant 1 to Plant 2.

Project Architecture & Methodology

From raw 15-minute telemetry to continuous sub-watt inverter power prediction.

ML Pipeline
Module 1 • Telemetry Data & EDA

15-minute inner-join alignment, daylight filtering, and diurnal irradiance profile mapping.

Module 2 • Supervised Regularized Regression

OLS Normal Equation vs GD, Ridge L2 condition stabilization, Lasso L1 sparsity, and Elastic Net.

Module 4 • Unsupervised Regimes & Augmentation

4 K-Means operational regimes, DBSCAN dropouts, and cluster centroid distance feature augmentation.

Module 5 • Validation Diagnostics & Transfer

Expanding TimeSeriesSplit, 5x3 Nested CV, synthetic spike testing, and cross-plant domain transfer.
