Continuous Physics vs. Classroom Classification Replacements

Why classification evaluation techniques were rejected for continuous solar AC power prediction.

Methodological Rigor
Classroom Technique Status Reason for Rejection in Continuous kW Forecasting Proper Continuous Regression Replacement
Platt Scaling & Isotonic Excluded Operate exclusively on classification probabilities $P(Y=1|X) \in [0, 1]$. Cannot calibrate continuous kW power. Residual Normality & Scale-Location Diagnostics
Reliability Diagrams Excluded Bins discrete probabilities against empirical event frequencies. Normal Q-Q Probability Plots (`scipy.stats.probplot`)
McNemar's 2x2 Test Excluded Requires a $2 \times 2$ contingency table of discordant binary decisions; thresholding kW destroys error magnitudes. Paired Bootstrap (B=1,000) 95% CIs & Paired t-Tests on $d_t = e_A^2 - e_B^2$
Unfiltered MAPE Replaced Night near-zero power ($y \approx 0$ kW) causes division-by-zero, exploding MAPE to 49,661%. Daylight-Filtered MAPE ($y > 10$ kW) yielding true 0.53% error
Random Shuffle K-Fold Excluded Shuffling sequential telemetry leaks future sensor states into past predictions (100% fold leak). Expanding-Window `TimeSeriesSplit` (Strict 0.0% Leakage)
